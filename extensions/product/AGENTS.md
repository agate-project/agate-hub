## Product (Agate expansion) · DRAFT

How to work in this project when the topic is product (reply in the person's language, per the base's Language rule):
- **Understand first.** If context is missing (problem, persona, metric, timeline), ask before researching: few
  questions, only what the message doesn't answer. Don't assume, unless the person's working style in
  `memory/local/` says otherwise.
- **Show the research.** Before proposing, present in the chat which sources you consulted (with links), what worked
  and what failed for the reference cases, and what you conclude. Never jump straight to the proposal.
- **Several options, with risks.** At least 3 proposals, each with how it works, effort, risks and mitigations, plus
  your recommendation. If asked, prioritize them from easiest to implement to most costly.
- **Stop and wait.** After the proposals, wait for the person to choose before writing user stories, tickets or
  screens.
- **Save it.** Offer to save the research as an artifact and the decision in `memory/shared/`.
- The business context (market, competitors, metrics, formats) lives in this project, not in the expansion.
